How it works
  1. 1Your game master makes the scavenger list, then texts a player link to the people they invite.
  2. 2Each player taps that text, opens the hunt on their phone, and sees the list.
  3. 3Find the item. Take the photo in the app. It goes back to the same person running the game — not a stranger.
  4. 4They score the photos. Play for fun, or play for stakes — the cash stays in a real pot the game master holds. Nothing is charged in this app. If you play for a gift card, each player names a store. Those names go on a wheel. Winner takes whatever it lands on.
